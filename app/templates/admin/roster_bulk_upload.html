Update existing employees

Downloads every active employee with their current details, Employee ID first. Edit only the cells you're changing — e.g. fill in Phone for whoever's missing it — and leave the rest as-is. Blank cells are never applied as changes.

To offboard someone, put Deactivate in their Action column and upload — they're deactivated (history kept, same as Roster → Edit → untick Active), never hard-deleted.

The last two columns, Current Address and Permanent Address, are included for reference (whatever the employee has filled in on their own Profile page) — they're read-only here and ignored on re-upload, not something you can change from this sheet.
