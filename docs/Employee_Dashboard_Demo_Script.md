## My Day

"This is the page you'll use the most. It's where you log your work every single day. Let's go through it in the order you'll actually use it."

### 1. Punch In and Break
**(Do this)** Point at the **Punch In** and **Start Break** buttons at the top of the page.

"Before anything else, at the very top you'll see **Punch In**. Click this when you start your workday — think of it as your attendance clock. It runs a countdown/timer so you can see how long you've been punched in."

"Next to it is **Start Break** — use this whenever you step away, lunch or otherwise. Click it again (it'll say **End Break**) when you're back. This is separate from your task log — it doesn't log any Project or Task, it's purely your attendance record and a personal live countdown for the day. The compensation numbers you'll see later on My Hours come from your logged task rows, not from Punch In/Out."

### 2. Plan for the Day
**(Do this)** Point at the **Plan for the Day** card.

"This is optional, but it's useful. Before you even start working, you can write down what you're planning to do — pick the Project, pick the Task, and you can even add an **estimated time** for how long you think it'll take."

"You can also plan a little ahead, not just today. Usually you can plan up to the end of this current work week. Once the week ends, the next thing you're allowed to plan is the following Monday. It's meant for quick planning, not a long calendar."

"If your Team Lead or admin assigns a task to you directly, it'll also show up here with a small **'assigned by'** tag — you can work it just like your own, but you can't delete an assigned one yourself."

### 3. Today's Plan
**(Do this)** Point at the **Today's Plan** list.

"Every plan you just added shows up here as a list. Each one shows its status — **Planned**, **Running**, **Paused**, or **Done** — with a little checkbox-style marker next to it."

"From here, you can hit **Start** right on a planned item — that kicks off a live timer for it, which brings us to the next part."

### 4. Auto time capture
**(Do this)** Show the Project and Task dropdowns, then Start.

"This is a live timer — think of it like a stopwatch. Pick your Project, pick your Task, hit **Start**, and it runs while you work. When you're done, hit **Stop**, and it saves that exact time as one row in your task log."

"If you get pulled away — a call, a meeting — hit **Pause**, and **Resume** when you're back. And one thing to know: if you leave a timer running for more than 4 hours straight, the system automatically splits it into 4-hour chunks for you. You don't have to do anything — it just keeps your log clean."

### 5. Add Task
**(Do this)** Show the Add Task form.

"This is the second way to log time — for typing something in after the fact. If you already know your start time and end time, just fill it in here: Project, Task, Start time, End time, and a short note about what you did."

"Use whichever works for you — the live timer while you work, or typing it in afterward. Most people mix both."

### 6. Today Tasks
**(Do this)** Point at the task log table and progress bar.

"This is your actual log for the day — every row you've logged, from the timer or typed in manually, shows up here with the time, plus a progress bar showing how close you are to your target hours."

"If you hover over the Duration number, it shows you the exact start and end time — we keep the table simple, but that detail is still right there when you need it."

"If there's a gap between two logged rows — say you forgot to log 30 minutes — you'll see a small warning row for it, with a **Fill** button that pre-fills the Add Task form for you."

### 7. Submit Day
**(Do this)** Point at the **Submit Day** button at the top of the page.

"Once you're done for the day, hit **Submit Day**. This locks the day so nothing on it can be edited by mistake — think of it as closing out your day."

"Two things to remember: first, your hours already count toward your target the moment you log them — Submit Day doesn't add anything new, it just locks the day, so don't stress if you forget to click it right away. Second, if something needs to change after you've submitted, you don't need to ask around — there's a **Request unlock** option right there that sends a note straight to your admin asking them to reopen it."

### 8. My Month (the small card)
**(Do this)** Point at the **My Month** card on the side.

"This gives you a quick glance at your logged hours and target for the whole month, plus a mini calendar showing which days you're ahead or behind — all without leaving this page. If you want the full detailed version, that's the My Hours page, which I'll show next."

### 9. Reminders
**(Do this)** Point at the **Reminders** card.

"This just surfaces anything you should keep an eye on — things like a pending overtime request, or your remaining Planned Time leave balance. It only shows up when there's actually something worth flagging."

## My Hours

**(Do this)** Click **My Hours** in the top menu.

"This is where you check your full numbers for the month — not just today."

### The KPI numbers at the top
"You'll see a few boxes:
- **Strikes this month** — how many days counted against you (missed or short days).
- **Running hours balance** — are you ahead or behind your target overall this month.
- **Compensation credit/owed** — this comes from your logged task rows too, same as the Running hours balance next to it, but it resets every month instead of running forever, and lets overtime elsewhere in the month automatically pay down a shortfall day.
- **Leave days** — how many of each leave type you've used this month."

### Hours ledger
**(Do this)** Click a week row, then click a day's "Entries" to expand it.

"Below that is the **Hours ledger**, organized by week. Click a week, and it opens into individual days, each with a Status — things like Complete, Partial, Missing, Leave, Overtime, Weekend, or Holiday. Click a day's **Entries**, and it opens up every task you logged that day, so you can double check everything without going back to My Day."

"This page is really your 'proof' page — if anyone ever asks 'how many hours did I log this month,' this is where the real answer lives."

---

## My Requests

**(Do this)** Click **My Requests ▾** to open the dropdown.

"This is a dropdown with everything related to leave and overtime."

### 1. Leave Request
**(Do this)** Click **Leave Request**.

"At the top, you'll see **Your leave balance** — how much of each leave type you have left. We have five leave types now: **Planned Time, Unplanned Time, Unpaid Time, Bereavement Time,** and **Special Paid Time**."

"Planned Time builds up automatically every month based on how long you've been with us — you don't request that, it just accrues. Unplanned Time is your general leave — for example, this covers up to 40 hours a year. Unpaid Time and Bereavement Time are there when you need them. Special Paid Time is only ever granted by management directly — you can't request it yourself."

"To ask for leave, scroll down to **Request leave** — pick your leave type, pick a date, and choose Full Day, Half Day, or a Custom number of hours. Submit it, and it goes into the approval process."

"Below that, **Your requests** shows a small progress tracker for each one — you'll see whether it's just Submitted, currently with your Team Lead, or already decided by the Super Admin. So you always know exactly where your request is sitting."

### 2. Overtime Request
**(Do this)** Click **Overtime Request**.

"This has two parts. First, **Request pre-approval** — if you know you're going to work extra hours, request it here ahead of time, or even after the fact for admin review."

"Second, there's **Request an Overtime ↔ Missed Hours match**. This is for a specific situation: say you fell short on one day, but worked extra on another day in the same month. You can request that your extra hours cover the shortfall, so it doesn't count against you. An admin reviews and approves it."

"Same as Leave, your requests here also show that progress tracker, so you always know where things stand."

### 3. Holidays
**(Do this)** Click **Holidays**, if visible on your screen.

"This just shows the full company holiday calendar — one shared list for everyone."

## Support

**(Do this)** Click **Support** in the top menu.

"If you have a question, or something isn't working right, don't message anyone directly — use this page so there's a record of it."

"Click **Ask a question**, type in what's going on, and hit **Send to admin**. It'll show up below in **Your questions**, along with the status and the admin's reply once they respond. You can always come back here to check if it's been answered."

---

## Profile

**(Do this)** Click your name/photo in the top-right corner.

"Last stop — your Profile. This is where all your personal information lives, and it's completely self-service, so you fill it in yourself whenever you're ready."

"At the top, you can upload a profile photo — that's the picture you'll see next to your name across the whole app."

"Below that, there are two cards. **Personal Details** — this covers your date of birth, blood type, family info, nationality, skills/hobbies, and all your contact numbers and addresses, all in one form. **Employment Details** — this is your bank account for payroll, plus PAN, Aadhaar, UAN, and ESI numbers. Any sensitive number you enter here is automatically masked everywhere it's shown afterward, so it's never displayed in full."

"Each card shows whether you've added your info yet or not, so you know at a glance what's still pending."

### Quick reference (for you, not to read aloud)

| Section | What it's for |
|---|---|
| My Day → Punch In & Break | Your attendance clock — separate from your task log; a personal live countdown only, doesn't feed the compensation numbers on My Hours (those come from your logged task rows) |
| My Day → Plan for the Day | Jot down what you're planning to work on, with an optional time estimate |
| My Day → Today's Plan | Your list of planned items; Start one to begin its timer |
| My Day → Auto time capture | Live Start/Pause/Stop timer for logging time in real time |
| My Day → Add Task | Type in a start/end time after the fact |
| My Day → Today Tasks | Your full log for the day, plus a progress bar |
| My Day → Submit Day | Locks the day; hours already counted before this — use Request unlock if you need it reopened |
| My Day → My Month (card) | Quick glance at this month's hours/target without leaving the page |
| My Day → Reminders | Surfaces anything worth flagging (pending requests, low leave balance, etc.) |
| My Hours | Monthly KPIs (strikes, balance, compensation, leave) and the week-by-week ledger |
| My Requests → Leave Request | Check leave balance, request leave, track request status |
| My Requests → Overtime Request | Request overtime pre-approval, request a missed-hours match |
| My Requests → Holidays | View the shared company holiday calendar |
| Support | Ask a question, track admin replies |
| Profile | Upload a photo, fill in Personal Details and Employment Details |

**Note:** if Ticketing is turned on for your environment, Support will also show a "Tickets" section for reporting bugs or feature ideas — it's off by default, so skip that part unless you've enabled it.
